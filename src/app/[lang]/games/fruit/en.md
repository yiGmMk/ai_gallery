## Free Online Fruit Slicing Game

Play a fruit slicing game right in your browser—no app download or installation needed. Hold and drag your mouse on a computer, or swipe with one finger on a phone or tablet. Slice the flying fruit, avoid the bombs, and see how many points you can score in one round.

This page offers Fruit Ninja-style browser gameplay. It is not a download page for the official Fruit Ninja app.

### How to Start Playing

1. Wait for the game and its opening animation to finish loading.
2. **Swipe through the watermelon marked “NEW GAME” in the middle of the menu.** A tap alone will not slice it.
3. Hold your mouse button or keep your finger on the screen as you swipe through flying fruit.
4. After game over, click or tap the screen to return to the menu, then slice the watermelon to start another round.

### Touch and Mouse Controls

| Device | How to slice |
| --- | --- |
| Phone or tablet | Press with one finger and swipe through fruit. Lift your finger to end the stroke, then press again for the next slice. |
| Computer | Hold the left mouse button and drag through fruit. Release the button to end the stroke. |

The game scales to fit the page width. Swiping inside the game controls the blade; to scroll down to this guide, swipe outside the game area. Only one finger is needed—there is no two-finger gesture to learn.

### Scoring and Game Over

- **Each sliced fruit is worth 1 point.** Cutting several fruits in one stroke adds one point for each fruit.
- **Hitting a bomb ends the round.** Check its position before making a wide swipe.
- **Missing 3 fruits ends the round.** The crosses in the upper-right corner track your misses.
- This version focuses on slicing fruit, avoiding bombs, and improving your score. It does not include selectable game modes, unlockable achievements, or a blade shop.

### Tips for a Higher Score

- **Choose a clear path.** If a bomb is close to a fruit, avoid sweeping across the whole screen just to catch it.
- **Watch the top of the arc.** Fruit usually moves more slowly near its highest point, giving you more time to aim.
- **Swipe through the target.** Horizontal, vertical, and diagonal strokes all work. A quick swipe should cross the fruit rather than stop beside it.
- **Lift between strokes.** Starting the next slice from a deliberate position can make bombs easier to avoid than holding down and chasing fruit.
- **Build consistency first.** Fewer misses and safer strokes matter more than trying to cut every fruit at once.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is this fruit slicing game free, and do I need to download it?

It is free to play on this page. Wait for the browser game to load and start playing—there is no app to install or payment required to unlock the game.

#### How do I start on my phone? Why does tapping the watermelon do nothing?

Wait for the opening animation to finish, then swipe with one finger from one side of the central watermelon to the other. The menu uses a slicing gesture: tapping or holding still will not cut it.

#### Can I play with a mouse instead of a touchscreen?

Yes. Hold the left mouse button while dragging through fruit, then release it to finish the stroke. Moving the mouse without holding the button does not slice fruit.

#### Is there a combo bonus for slicing several fruits at once?

Each fruit is worth 1 point. A single stroke can slice several fruits, but this version does not add a combo multiplier or bonus points.

#### Why did my round suddenly end?

You either hit a bomb or reached 3 missed fruits. Watch for bombs and check the miss indicators in the upper-right corner before trying another round.
